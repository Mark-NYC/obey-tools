The question beneath the question

The earliest Christian preaching centered on the claim that Jesus was raised from the dead. Paul passed on a tradition of appearances in 1 Corinthians 15, and the Gospels describe the empty tomb and encounters with Jesus. Historians can evaluate these claims, but they disagree about which explanation best accounts for them.

It's worth asking what we can reasonably establish from ancient sources, how miracles should be evaluated, and whether alternative explanations fit the evidence. Christian faith does not require treating every objection as foolish. The resurrection is a claim worth investigating carefully.
