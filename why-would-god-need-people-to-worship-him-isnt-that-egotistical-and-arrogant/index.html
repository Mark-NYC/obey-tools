The question beneath the question

Christians have long held that God is not dependent on us. Worship is not supposed to feed divine insecurity or keep God emotionally satisfied. Instead, it is understood as a response to what is worthy of love, gratitude, and wonder.

That still leaves a good question: when can worship become manipulation or pressure? Healthy Christian worship should never silence honest questions. It should draw people toward love of God and neighbor, not away from their humanity.
