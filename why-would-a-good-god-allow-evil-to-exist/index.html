Why would a good God allow evil to exist?

Why would a loving, powerful God permit cruelty and injustice?

The question beneath the question

The problem of evil asks whether a perfectly good and powerful God could have good reasons to permit the wrongs we see. Free choice is one response Christians offer, especially for evil caused by people. But it does not neatly explain natural disasters, illness, or the suffering of children.

The Bible does not ask us to call evil good. It names injustice, allows lament, and presents the cross as God entering human suffering. Christian hope is not that evil is unreal, but that evil is opposed by God and will not have the final word.

Talk it through

Three questions worth discussing

  1. Which kinds of evil make belief in God most difficult for you?
  2. How far does human freedom help explain evil, and where does it fall short?
  3. What difference does Jesus' suffering make to this question?
