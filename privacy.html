Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

OBEY.TOOLS is operated by Multiplying Disciples. This policy covers our website and iPhone app. Contact us at contact@multiplyingdisciples.us.

Using the tools without an account

You can use the core tools without creating an account. The tools store information you enter on your device, including names or nicknames, conversation notes, follow-up steps, church assessments, and study progress. The iPhone app also keeps a local backup in its private app storage, which may be included in device backups. We do not receive this locally stored content unless you choose to sign in and sync it or otherwise share it.

Accounts and syncing

If you create an account, we use Supabase to handle authentication and store your email address, account details, and synced tool data. Supabase stores passwords in hashed form. Depending on which features you use, synced information can include conversation notes, progress, church assessments, networks, checklists, invitations, and session summaries. Our Supabase project is hosted in the United States.

Optional location and maps

Some Conversation Box milestones can contribute to a shared progress map. If you grant location access, location information may be processed by Supabase or OpenStreetMap Nominatim to identify an approximate place. The map stores rounded or approximate locations and milestone details rather than the names of people in your Conversation Box. Map providers, including Esri/ArcGIS, may receive technical information such as your IP address and map area when tiles load. You can deny or disable location access in your device settings.

Website analytics and third-party content

The website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and usage. The iPhone app build removes Google Analytics and does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Optional videos and forms may be provided by YouTube and Google Forms, which process information under their own policies.

Sharing and service providers

We use service providers to run the tools and maps. Information you choose to submit to shared networks or checklists may be accessible to other authorized participants. We do not sell your personal data. Technical data may also be processed by hosting, map, and video providers as needed to deliver their services.

Your choices and deletion

The iPhone app provides a local data export in Settings. Signed-in users can request account deletion from Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes the account and linked cloud data, subject to the need to transfer ownership of shared networks or checklists first. Some shared records may remain without your account identifier to preserve collaborators' work. Data stored locally on your iPhone is retained when you delete your cloud account; uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to device backups. For help with deletion or access requests, email contact@multiplyingdisciples.us. Backups and service-provider records may be retained for limited periods under their operational policies.

Children

OBEY.TOOLS is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the tools change. The date above shows the latest revision. Questions: contact@multiplyingdisciples.us.
