The question beneath the question

The conquest narratives are among the Bible’s hardest passages. Some scholars argue that ancient Near Eastern war language used exaggeration and must be read alongside texts showing Canaanites continuing to live in the land. Others question whether those explanations resolve the moral problem.

No interpretation should turn these passages into permission for violence today. Reading carefully requires attention to genre, historical context, the dignity of human beings, and Jesus’ teaching about enemies. Serious Christians disagree on how to reconcile every detail.
