The question beneath the question

Christian teaching about judgment raises hard questions: What is the nature of hell? What about people who have never heard of Jesus? How should we understand justice and mercy? Christians agree that judgment is serious, but they differ on important details, including how biblical imagery should be interpreted.

Jesus speaks frankly about judgment while also welcoming the lost and offering forgiveness. A thoughtful conversation needs to hold those claims together rather than use fear as a shortcut to belief.
