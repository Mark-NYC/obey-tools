The question beneath the question

Christians worship Jesus as more than a prophet or moral teacher. In the Gospels, he forgives sins, speaks of unique authority, and receives responses that go beyond respect for an ordinary rabbi. John presents Jesus as God's eternal Word who became human.

Some readers interpret those passages differently, and it's important to distinguish the text of Jesus' words from later theological explanations. Investigate Jesus' actions and claims in their Jewish context, then ask whether the Christian conclusion makes sense of them.
