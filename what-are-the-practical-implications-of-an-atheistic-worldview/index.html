The question beneath the question

Atheism means not believing in a god; it does not require one single view of morality or life's meaning. Atheists can ground ethics in human well-being, relationships, reason, or social obligations, and can live generous and purposeful lives.

Some Christian philosophers argue that objective moral obligations ultimately require God; others dispute that conclusion. A good conversation distinguishes whether someone can act morally from the separate question of what makes moral truths objective. This featured video presents one Christian argument, not an uncontested description of atheists.
