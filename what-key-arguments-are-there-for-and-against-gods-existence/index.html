The question beneath the question

Arguments for God’s existence often focus on why anything exists at all, the apparent order of the universe, and whether moral truths point beyond human opinion. None of these arguments automatically establishes every claim of Christianity, but each invites a serious conversation about what best explains reality.

Skeptics challenge whether a first cause must be personal, whether design is a stronger explanation than natural processes, and whether suffering counts as evidence against a good God. It is worth hearing those objections rather than treating the question as settled by one argument.
