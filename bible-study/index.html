Your next step

Come this Wednesday.

We meet Wednesdays from 7–8 PM Eastern in a Messenger group video call.

  1. Message us to join. Go back to your conversation with Hope for NYC on Facebook and tell us you’d like to come.
  2. We’ll confirm with you. With your permission, we’ll add you to our Messenger group chat 10–15 minutes before the gathering.
  3. Join the call in the chat. At 7 PM, open the group conversation and join the video call.

You can simply reply:

“I’d like to join Wednesday’s Bible study. You can add me to the Messenger chat before the call.”

Have a friend who’s curious too? Invite them to come with you.

A few questions

Do I need to be a Christian?

No. Whether you follow Jesus, are exploring faith, or aren’t sure what you believe, you’re welcome to read and discover with us.

What should I bring?

A phone with Messenger, or a computer where you can access your Facebook messages. If you have a Bible, bring it. If you don’t, we’ll read the passage together.

Will I have to know all the answers?

No one needs to have it all figured out. We’ll look at the passage together, ask questions, and listen to one another.

Can I come if I haven’t joined before?

Yes. You can start with us this Wednesday. No previous study or preparation needed.

What happens between Wednesdays?

After your first gathering, you’re welcome to stay connected in the Messenger chat, get to know the group, and read short Bible passages with us during the week.
