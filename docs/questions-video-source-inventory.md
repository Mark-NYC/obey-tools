## Page pattern

1. Question in the original WordPress permalink; dark hero and on-page video.
2. Clear current-video attribution.
3. Fresh, original short companion notes, **not** a transcription or reconstructed old answer.
4. Three open-ended discussion prompts that avoid pressuring a predetermined answer.
5. Scripture references for reading in context, not isolated proof-text quotes.
6. Two related questions and accessible share action.
