The question beneath the question

Science is remarkably good at investigating how the natural world works through observation, experiment, and testable explanations. Christian faith also makes claims about reality, including creation, human purpose, and miracles. Sometimes these questions overlap and sometimes they address different kinds of explanation.

Christians disagree among themselves about some interpretations of Genesis and scientific findings. Asking whether science and faith conflict requires naming the specific claim and evidence. A scientist's commitment to methodological natural explanations is not identical to a philosophical claim that nothing beyond nature exists.
