The question beneath the question

The existence of many religions doesn't automatically make every claim equally true or equally false. Different traditions disagree about God, Jesus, salvation, suffering, and what happens after death. A fair comparison starts by understanding each tradition as its followers understand it.

Christianity centers on the person and resurrection of Jesus and on salvation as God's gift rather than simply an achievement of moral effort. That is a claim to examine, not a reason to mock another faith. Ask what historical and philosophical evidence supports or challenges the central claims.
