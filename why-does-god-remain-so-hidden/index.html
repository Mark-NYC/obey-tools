The question beneath the question

For some people the difficulty is intellectual: they want clearer evidence. For others it is personal: they have prayed through a painful season and heard nothing back. Those are different forms of the same question, and neither should be dismissed.

Christian faith claims that God has made himself known in creation, Israel’s story, and ultimately Jesus. That does not erase the experience of silence. Even biblical writers ask why God seems far away. The question invites both honest searching and attention to what we expect a relationship with God to look like.
