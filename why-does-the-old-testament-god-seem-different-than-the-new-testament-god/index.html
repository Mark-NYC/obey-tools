The question beneath the question

The contrast can seem stark when readers move from Old Testament war and judgment to Jesus teaching love of enemies. Yet the Old Testament repeatedly describes God as merciful and patient, while the New Testament also speaks of judgment.

Understanding the Bible means asking what each passage is doing, who first heard it, and how the different parts of the story fit together. Christians read the character of God through the life of Jesus without pretending the difficult texts disappear.
