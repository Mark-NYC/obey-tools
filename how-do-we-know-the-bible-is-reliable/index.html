The question beneath the question

The Bible is not one book written at one time. It is a collection of texts written across centuries in different genres. Reliability involves several distinct questions: how carefully the text was transmitted, what historical claims are corroborated, and how we should interpret what each author meant.

We have many ancient manuscript witnesses to the New Testament, including differences that scholars study openly. That matters for reconstructing the text, but it does not by itself prove every event happened. The best next step is to examine a specific claim, its sources, and the strongest arguments on both sides.
